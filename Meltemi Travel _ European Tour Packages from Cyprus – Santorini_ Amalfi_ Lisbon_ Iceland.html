How much does a European trip from Cyprus cost?

Our packages start at €140 per person for a two-day Troodos weekend and reach €1,090 for five days in Iceland. Prices include VAT, hotels, breakfast and a local guide, plus return flights on trips that need one.

When is the best time to see the Northern Lights in Iceland?

The best time is from September to March, when nights are long and dark. Our Iceland trip includes a guided aurora hunt, and clear skies matter more than the exact date.

Which documents do I need for these trips?

Bring a valid passport or ID card; rules depend on your nationality and residence status. Add visa support to your booking and we check your documents before you pay the balance.

Is my money protected when I book?

Yes. Our packages fall under the EU Package Travel Directive, which includes insolvency protection and your right to a refund or repatriation.
